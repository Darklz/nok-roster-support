NOK Payment Checker

Support and setup instructions for Nok Air pilots who have received the app link.

Getting Started

  1. Install the app. In Settings, find Safari and open Extensions.
  2. Enable NOK Payment Checker and allow the website access it requests for the roster and rate service.
  3. Sign in to Merlot in Safari and open your complete roster document.
  4. Open the Safari extensions menu, select NOK Payment Checker and tap Read Roster.
  5. Review the loaded report. If your rates are missing, check the employee ID shown, complete all six rate fields and tap Submit Rates and Calculate. An internet connection is required.

Pay Rates and Reference Hours

The system provides your configured pay rates and reference hours for each route. Open Edit Calculation Rates to adjust salary, fixed allowance, hourly rates, transport, layover and social security for the current report. Withholding tax and monthly other income can also be adjusted. Report changes do not update your saved rate record and reset when you read a new roster. Contact your group administrator to update saved rates or reference hours.

Flight pay follows the flights listed in your roster Profile and the reference hours for each route. Missing reference hours appear in red. Actual duty hours are used only where eligible flight hours can be identified correctly. If the required hours are unavailable, the report marks the total as incomplete.

Daily Details and Draft Payslip

Each day shows Flight, Transport and Lay Over payments, together with the hourly rates applied. Layover nights follow accommodation entries in the roster; crossing midnight alone does not count as a layover. The default layover rate is THB 500 per night. Rates may differ according to your saved configuration.

The DRAFT ONLY payslip shows estimated gross pay, deductions and net pay. Tax and social security are estimates and can be adjusted. It is not an employer-issued payslip. The expected payment message refers to the end of the month following your roster month.

Troubleshooting

  • Cannot read a roster: Check that the complete document is open in Safari and that the extension has website access. After an app update, close and reopen the roster tab.
  • Cannot retrieve rates: Check your internet connection and extension permissions. If the problem continues, contact your group administrator.
  • Reference hours are unavailable or invalid: Ask your administrator to check the route configuration.
  • Correct or delete your rate record: Contact your group administrator privately. Uninstalling the app does not delete records held by the rate service.

Privacy and Support

The rate service uses your employee ID and configured pay rates. Please read the Privacy Policy before submitting information, including the service’s access limitations.

Report a general issue

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post employee IDs, salaries, real rosters, payslips, passwords or other personal information. Use fictional examples and remove personal information from screenshots. Send requests about your personal rate record to your group administrator privately.
