Privacy Policy

Nok Roster Reader · Developer: San Jeepet
Effective date: 25 October 2026

Data processed on your device

When you choose to read a roster, the Safari extension reads the roster document already open in your authorized Merlot session. This can include employee code, crew profile text, dates, duties, flight numbers, routes, flight and duty times, and related roster details. Salary and pay rates you enter are used locally to estimate income. The app does not ask for or collect your Merlot password.

No developer collection or tracking

The app and extension do not transmit roster data, employee details, salary or pay rates to a developer-operated server. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or sale of data. Processing and storage are local to your device. The demo uses synthetic data without real employee identities.

Storage and retention

The extension stores the most recently read roster and configured rates in local extension storage. Reading another roster replaces the saved roster. The in-app demo stores its settings separately in local web storage. Disabling the extension does not necessarily erase data. App deletion and applicable Safari website/extension data controls can remove locally stored data; Apple device backups and app offloading may retain data according to your device settings.

Permissions and third-party services

Safari access is requested for ddportal.merlot.aero so the extension can read the roster you choose. Merlot login and browsing remain governed by your employer's and Merlot provider's policies. App Store downloads, diagnostics you elect to share with Apple, iOS backups and Apple platform services are governed by Apple's settings and privacy policies.

Support requests

If you voluntarily contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and is publicly visible in that repository. Do not include passwords, employee identifiers, payroll details or real roster data. The developer uses information you submit to respond and troubleshoot. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to its service.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer through Nok Roster Reader Support. Please use synthetic examples and remove personal information. Updates to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
