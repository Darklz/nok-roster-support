Information Used by the Rate Service

When you read a roster, the extension sends the employee ID selected from the document to the administrator-managed rate service. The system returns your configured salary, fixed allowance, hourly rates, daily transport, layover rate, social security, withholding percentage and reference route hours.

If rates are missing, the submission form sends that employee ID and six entered pay amounts to create a rate record. The administrator may also add or update records. These records include the employee ID, financial settings, active status and revision information. Financial information is linked to your identity through the employee ID.

Access Limitations

The service does not require a login or activation code. Anyone who knows an employee ID and the service address can retrieve that ID’s salary and pay rates, or submit initial rates for an ID without an existing rate record. Selecting an ID from a roster does not verify ownership. Receiving an app link or using unlisted distribution does not protect the confidentiality of rate records.

Existing configured rates cannot be overwritten through the initial submission form. Corrections to saved rates are made by the administrator.

Information Kept on Your Device

Other roster details, crew names, duty dates, flight numbers, routes and flight times are processed in device memory for the current reading. Full roster documents, Merlot passwords and cookies are not uploaded to the rate service. Local report adjustments, calculation results and draft payslips are not sent back to the service. Reading another roster resets report adjustments. Older app versions may have retained local records.

Purpose and Service Providers

Employee IDs and financial settings are used for app functionality: retrieving and maintaining rates and estimating flight pay. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not use these records for cross-app or cross-website tracking, and does not sell them.

The administrator’s rate system is hosted using Google cloud services and communicates over HTTPS. Google may process technical connection information under its Privacy Policy. The extension requests access to the Merlot document website and the service domains needed to retrieve rates. Merlot, Apple and GitHub operate under their own policies.

Retention, Corrections and Deletion

Rate records remain in the system until the administrator changes or deletes them. Disabling the extension or uninstalling the app does not delete remote records. Contact the group administrator privately to access, correct, disable or delete your record. Disabling a record is not the same as deleting it. Local data from older versions and device backups are managed through the relevant app, Safari and Apple settings.

Contact

For general issues, visit Support. GitHub Issues are public: do not post employee IDs, financial details, actual rosters, payslips, passwords or personal information. Contact your group administrator through your existing private communication channel for requests about your rate record.
